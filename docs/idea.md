Integrated Dealership Management System (DMS)
A comprehensive cloud-based dealership management system forms the foundation of modern motorcycle dealership operations. Key features should include:
Core DMS Capabilities
	•	Inventory Management: Real-time tracking of motorcycles, parts, and accessories with automated stock alerts and reorder points
	•	Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Complete 360-degree customer view with lead management, sales pipeline tracking, and customer history
	•	Sales Management: Digital sales processes from inquiry to delivery, including online booking and payment processing
	•	Service Management: Comprehensive workshop management with appointment scheduling, job cards, and technician assignment
	•	Financial Integration: Automated invoicing, accounting integration with popular platforms like Xero and QuickBooks, and financial reportin
